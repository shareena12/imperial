Welcome to IIAMS

Imperial Institute of Advanced Medical Sciences (IIAMS), Kottakkal is an emerging center of excellence in Allied Health Sciences, committed to shaping the next generation of healthcare professionals through innovation, precision, and global standards.

At IIAMS, we go beyond conventional education. We integrate academic excellence with clinical exposure, advanced technologies, and research-driven learning methodologies—creating a transformative ecosystem that mirrors global institutions.

What is Allied Health Science?

Allied Health Sciences represent the backbone of modern healthcare systems.

Vision

To establish IIAMS as a globally recognized institution in Allied Health Sciences comparable to international standards by fostering excellence in education, research, and clinical practice.

Mission

To deliver industry-aligned, future-ready healthcare education. To integrate global best practices into Indian healthcare training. To empower students with practical, real-world clinical exposure

Academic Excellence

Outcome-based education model Advanced lab and simulation facilities. Integrated clinical training programs. Continuous skill assessment and mentorship. International-standard curriculum alignment
